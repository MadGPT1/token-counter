What is a token?

Models don't read characters or words. They read tokens: chunks of text that are often a short word or part of a longer one. In English, one token is about four characters, or roughly three quarters of a word.

Why do other languages use more tokens?

Tokenizers are trained mostly on English. Cyrillic, Arabic, Hindi and similar scripts usually take about twice as many tokens per word, and Chinese, Japanese and Korean take roughly one token per character.

Why does the token count matter?

It sets two limits: how much text fits into one conversation (the context window) and how much an API request costs.
